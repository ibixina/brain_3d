Modifications by Cerebra (2026-10-19): binary label masks converted to indexed
triangle surfaces using marching cubes at 0.5, normals computed, coordinates
reoriented and recentered for an independent 3D view, geometry packed and
gzip-compressed, and display colors softened. No smoothing or resampling.
The supplied categorical labels and lookup table remain unchanged; the
original NIfTI bytes are retained in losslessly gzipped form. Probability
distributions were not used to recompute labels or confidence values.

Original papers, figures, and software retain their own rights. No study
figures, paper text, or toolbox code is included. This is a derived educational
atlas display, not an individual anatomical reconstruction or a clinical tool.
