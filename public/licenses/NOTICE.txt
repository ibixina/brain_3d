Cerebra — Third-party attributions

Geometry: modified visualization derivatives of FreeSurfer fsaverage data.
Copyright and terms: /licenses/freesurfer.txt
Sources: https://surfer.nmr.mgh.harvard.edu/ and the checksummed sources
listed in /models/atlas.json. Meshes are transformed, parcel-split, sampled,
and extracted from smoothed label volumes; they are not original data files.

Standalone brainstem: modified derivatives of FreeSurfer's Brainstem.zip
probability atlas. Maximum-probability labels (including background),
0.5 isosurfaces without extra smoothing or resampling, recentered display.
These are separate from the fsaverage model and the MRI templates.
Source, labels, method, frame, and checksums: /subregions/brainstem-provenance.json
Data terms: /licenses/freesurfer.txt
Iglesias et al. (2015): https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2015.02.065

Standalone thalamus: modified winner-surface derivatives of FreeSurfer's
Thalamus.zip probability atlas, without extra smoothing or resampling.
Source labels without winner voxels are retained as metadata, without meshes.
Separate from fsaverage and the MRI templates. Data terms: /licenses/freesurfer.txt
Source, method, frame, and checksums: /subregions/thalamus-provenance.json
Iglesias et al. (2018): https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2018.08.012

Standalone amygdala: modified winner-surface derivatives of FreeSurfer's
HippoAmyg.zip probability atlas. Only nine amygdala channels per side are
surfaced after winner competition across all 30 channels. Disconnected
portions are retained, without extra smoothing or resampling. The source
supplies reflected left/right probability grids. Separate from fsaverage
and MRI templates. Data terms: /licenses/freesurfer.txt
Source, method, frame, and checksums: /subregions/amygdala-provenance.json
Saygin, Kliemann et al. (2017): https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2017.04.046
