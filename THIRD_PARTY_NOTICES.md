The surfaces are extracted from the supplied atlas probabilities; this app does not run the paper’s participant-segmentation algorithm. - Source and both exact NIfTI files, hashes, 0.25 mm grids, reflected affines, and retained floating-point probability behavior match the amygdala audit above. Head/body, layer, tail, transition-area, white-matter, and fissure-space labels are not 20 cellular subfields. - Original study: Iglesias, Augustinack, Nguyen and colleagues (2015), *A computational atlas of the hippocampal formation using ex vivo, ultra-high resolution MRI: Application to adaptive segmentation of in vivo MRI*, NeuroImage 115, 117–137, [DOI 10.1016/j.neuroimage.2015.04.042](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2015.04.042). Concise original explanations follow the [author manuscript’s Table 2](https://www.nmr.mgh.harvard.edu/~iglesias/pdf/subfieldsNeuroimage2015preprint.pdf#page=7) and [current protocol](https://surfer.nmr.mgh.harvard.edu/fswiki/HippocampalSubfieldsAndNucleiOfAmygdala). Paper prose, figures, original histology/MRI, participant data, and segmentation software are not redistributed or run.
- Maximum-probability masks retain source-order ties, with marching cubes at 0.5 and no additional smoothing, resampling, or component cleanup. The source arrays are identical with reflected affines; winding is corrected for the right reflection. Source RAS coordinates become `[R,S,-A]` and are jointly recentered for an independent view, not registered to fsaverage or the bundled asymmetric MRI templates. A grid spacing is not microscopic accuracy. The fissure has 155 six-connected voxel groups per side and alveus has 108; these are mask statistics, not neuron counts or a complete anatomical extent.
- Runtime geometry is split by hemisphere, without duplicating a full-atlas asset. Parts use offsets within their matching hemisphere file. Each file has its own raw/compressed hashes; top-level geometry hashes are over asset bytes concatenated in listed order. Complete source names, per-file affines/hashes, label inclusion/exclusion, probability hashes, component counts, method, frame/recentering, and date are in [public/subregions/hippocampus-provenance.json](public/subregions/hippocampus-provenance.json).

## FreeSurfer probability evidence summaries

The four `public/subregions/*-evidence.json` files are **modified statistical derivatives** of the exact brainstem, thalamus, hippocampus, and amygdala probability files documented above. The same FreeSurfer February 2011 Part B data terms, full license, and General Hospital Corporation (MGH) attribution apply. No institution endorses these derivatives.

`scripts/build_subregion_evidence.py` validates the pinned archive/NIfTI hashes and channel tables before deriving all-channel winner counts, four bins of winning-voxel label probability, and a single native-grid peak sample with its strongest competing label. Source-order ties are preserved; source float32 probabilities are not resampled, renormalized, or thresholded. The end histogram bins retain source floating-point tolerance. Peak coordinates are zero-based native voxel indices and source RAS millimeters, not recentered display coordinates or a registration to the bundled MRI templates. Summary files retain the source-manifest checksum, archive/NIfTI hashes, method, date, original data/study URLs, license, and attribution. The original probability volumes, scans/histology, software, prose, and figures are not bundled. These summaries are not patient-specific segmentation results or calibrated measures of boundary accuracy.
